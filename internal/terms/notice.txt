Astrona CLI usage terms and risk notice
Revision: 2026-11-10

Astrona runs lab software on your machine. It can execute downloaded scripts,
change Kubernetes contexts and networks, and create or delete containers, virtual
machines and lab files. Bugs, unsafe lab content or mistakes can cause data loss,
service disruption or damage to your environment. Setup can install tools and
prepare WSL on Windows.

Use a disposable learning environment. Keep backups of important data, keep
production credentials out of labs, and review lab content before running it.
You are responsible for deciding whether a lab is safe for your environment.

While you are signed in and a lab runs, astrona sends that lab's CPU and memory
use to your Astrona lab session, about every 15 seconds, to show it on your labs
page. Run with --local-only, or turn it off with `astrona settings`, to send
nothing.
